What payment methods are accepted?

Different payment methods may be available depending on the service. Common methods include cards and online payments like jazzcash, easypaisa, upaisa etc.

How can I request a refund?

You can contact the support team and provide your payment details and reason and proof of fund for the refund request.

Where can I find my invoice?

Your invoice can usually be found in your account's billing or payment section.
